Quality & certifications

Quality you can verify

Eltek Industries operates an ISO 9001:2015 certified quality management system, and our conduit pipes carry the ISI mark for conformity to IS 9537.

How we build quality in

From compound to dispatch

  1. 01

    Virgin PVC compound

    Conduits are made from a controlled PVC compound with fire-retardant and UV-stabilising additives, so every batch performs the same way on site.

  2. 02

    In-house testing

    Samples from production are checked in our own lab for:

    • {{@icon impact "h-5 w-5 text-accent"}}Impact
    • {{@icon gauge "h-5 w-5 text-accent"}}Compression
    • {{@icon sun "h-5 w-5 text-accent"}}Heat resistance
    • {{@icon flame "h-5 w-5 text-accent"}}Fire behaviour
  3. 03

    Process control

    Extrusion temperatures, line speed and dimensions are monitored through each run, keeping outer diameter and wall thickness consistent — the basis of our ISO 9001:2015 quality system.

  4. 04

    Packing & dispatch

    Products are bundled and labelled size-wise for easy counting at the counter and on site, then dispatched from our SIDCUL Pantnagar plant. Pack details: [Packing details — bundle sizes, labels].
