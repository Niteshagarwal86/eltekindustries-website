About Eltek Industries

Engineering Safer Connections

From the industrial hub of SIDCUL Pantnagar, Uttarakhand, we make the PVC conduit systems that keep India's wiring safe — in homes, buildings and plants.

The founder

{{ site.founder }} {{ site.founderTitle }}

A visionary industrialist from Uttarakhand, {{ site.founder }} founded Eltek Industries with a simple conviction: the wiring hidden inside India's walls deserves the same care as anything we can see.

He set out to bring world-class, ISI-certified PVC electrical products to Indian homes and industry — and chose to build them in his home state, at the industrial hub of SIDCUL Pantnagar.

Under his leadership, Eltek has grown around quality systems, dependable supply and long-term relationships with electricians, dealers and project teams.
